## The starting point

Brooke brought experience as a former lead Montessori teacher in Vietnam, a children's author and a pet-sitting business owner. She wanted to explore how her teaching background, storytelling and work with animals could support a new offering in Santa Barbara, California.

The work started with discussion: what experience could she build on, which interests belonged together, who might benefit, and what would a manageable first version look like? Her supplied professional history informed the positioning; unspecified certifications, business details and dates remained visible confirmation items.

## What I helped her develop

- **Professional history and strengths:** Organized her supplied experience and identified connections among teaching, authorship, pet care and business ownership. Used those connections to discuss a possible direction without inventing qualifications.
- **Business idea and scope:** Developed Reading with Sage, a proposed picture-book and feelings program for children ages 4–7 with caregivers. Narrowed the launch area to Santa Barbara, Goleta and Carpinteria and kept financial tools out of the MVP.
- **Program design:** Planned six approximately 45-minute visits with a different primary book each time. Defined each opening, story invitation, read-aloud, movement break, lesson, caregiver practice and closing, with specific minute ranges and flexible participation.
- **Launch and communication:** Researched prospective libraries and community organizations, prepared library and nonprofit pitches, drafted follow-ups and social posts, and made a short guide with concrete next steps. Prospective venues are leads, not confirmed partners.
- **LinkedIn and professional positioning:** Prepared headline options, an About section, an Experience entry and a short business bio for Brooke's pet-sitting business. This is profile copy, not a claim that her LinkedIn account was created or updated.
- **Working MVP:** Built and published a workspace for library outreach, bookings, six-session series, reusable curriculum, parent sheets, tasks and launch materials. The site opens without an app login and offers JSON backup import/export.

## Result and practical limits

Brooke has a proposed program, a concrete session structure, ready-to-use communication drafts, professional profile copy and a published workspace she can try. This demonstrates discovery, synthesis, business planning, curriculum design, writing and MVP delivery; it does not establish bookings, program delivery, business growth or measured outcomes for children.

The earlier program-workspace MVP's records save in each visitor's browser. They survive normal closing and reopening, but do not sync across devices and can be lost if site data is cleared. JSON exports provide manual backups. Permanent cloud storage and automatic recovery are not configured for that separate MVP. The library-program website editor uses cloud storage for page text and photos. Editor saves are not automatically mirrored into this GitHub repository.

Sage's evaluation and registration for visits with children were confirmed by Brooke. Her exact professional certification and issuer were not supplied. Outreach and social content were drafted; no messages or posts were sent as part of this work.
